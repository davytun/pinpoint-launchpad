# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Investors use the portal to submit an application, complete their profile, and later discover vetted opportunities. Pinpoint staff use the administration area to review applications, activate accounts, and maintain oversight. Their work is sensitive and trust-based, so each screen should make status and next actions immediately clear.

## Product Purpose

Pinpoint Investment Network provides a controlled, compliant path from investor onboarding to curated startup dealflow. The portal keeps Pinpoint in the loop while reducing manual administration and avoiding off-platform activity.

## Brand Personality

Calm, modern, and authoritative. The interface should feel discreet and deliberate, with the restraint appropriate for investment and compliance workflows.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic fintech dashboards, noisy sales funnels, visual novelty that obscures decisions, and any UI that departs from the established Pinpoint founder and admin patterns.

## Design Principles

- Preserve trust through clear states, explicit permissions, and plain-language next steps.
- Extend existing Pinpoint patterns before introducing new interface conventions.
- Keep sensitive workflows focused, quiet, and free of distracting decoration.
- Make review and activation decisions easy to understand and hard to perform accidentally.
- Provide accessible feedback for every submitted, pending, approved, or rejected state.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.1 AA as a baseline. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, semantic form labels and errors, sufficient contrast, and reduced motion preferences.
